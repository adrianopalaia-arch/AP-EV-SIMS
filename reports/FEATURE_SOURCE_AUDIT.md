# Feature/source audit

Every feature in every requested market contract is inventoried in outputs/feature_source_audit.json. No contract feature is enabled for a qualifying historical backtest yet.
CFBD PPA remains PPA; it is not silently renamed EPA. Opponent-adjusted EPA requires a verified definition and source or an independently trained, time-safe EPA construction.
Current CFBD game/advanced/Elo captures only prove availability now. Retrospective CORE, season-end ratings, later injuries and observed postgame weather cannot qualify.
Missingness for a complete historical eligible universe is unknown, not zero. Optional historical weather is excluded if the forecast revision cannot be proven.
Feature and provider cost/incremental contribution are unmeasured. No new paid source is recommended from these empty results.
